# MERCIA - Business Management System

A comprehensive web-based business management solution for small and mid-level businesses.

## Project Overview

MERCIA is a professional, modern business management system designed to help entrepreneurs and business owners manage:
- 📦 **Inventory Management** - Track stock across multiple warehouses
- 💰 **Billing & Invoicing** - Professional invoices and payment tracking
- 👥 **Employee Management** - Attendance, payroll, and team management
- 📊 **Analytics & Reports** - Data-driven insights and custom dashboards
- 🛒 **Sales Management** - Order tracking and sales pipeline management
- ⚙️ **Customizable Settings** - Flexible workflows and integrations

## Color Palette

The website uses a carefully balanced 3-color scheme following design theory principles:
- **Primary (60%)**: #2C363F - Dark Navy - Used for headers, text, and structure
- **Secondary (30%)**: #F2F5EA - Cream/Off-White - Used for light backgrounds and spacing
- **Accent (10%)**: #E39774 - Warm Orange - Used for CTAs, highlights, and emphasis

## Customization

To customize the website:

1. **Colors** - Update the hex color codes in the `<style>` sections
2. **Content** - Edit text directly in the HTML files
3. **Images** - Replace emoji placeholders with actual images
4. **Links** - Update href attributes to match your actual URLs
5. **Forms** - Connect contact form to your backend service
