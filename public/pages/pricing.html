Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my plan anytime? +
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and we'll prorate your billing accordingly.
Is there a free trial? +
Absolutely! All plans come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. You get full access to all features during the trial period.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, and bank transfers. All payments are processed securely through industry-standard payment gateways.
Is there a contract or long-term commitment? +
No! MERCIA is completely month-to-month with no long-term contracts. You can cancel anytime, and we'll help you export your data.
Do you offer volume discounts? +
Yes, we do! For Enterprise plans and large deployments, we offer custom pricing. Contact our sales team for a personalized quote.
